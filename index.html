Spray the Fly

Two flies and a cockroach have moved into the lounge room. You've got one can of Mortein.

🎟️ The lowest spray count on the leaderboard wins a prize at the Vestacy Conference, Port Stephens!!
  • Use mouse to aim or the four arrow keys ← ↑ ↓ → to aim.
  • W A S D to walk, or tap W once to keep walking hands-free (tap again or press S to stop). The bugs run and fly away when you get close, so corner them.
  • Click (or press Space) to spray. The spray reaches about two metres in a narrow cone.
  • Spray as many times as you like. The game ends when all three bugs are down. Fewer sprays put you higher on the leaderboard; a faster time breaks ties.

Needs a keyboard and a mouse or trackpad.
